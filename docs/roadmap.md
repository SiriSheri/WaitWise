# Future Product & Technical Roadmap

## Overview
This document outlines future enhancements planned for WaitWise as the platform transitions from an MVP to enterprise deployment.

---

## Phase 1: Communication & Accessibility
- [ ] **SMS Turn Notifications**: Integration with Twilio / AWS SNS to send automated SMS text messages when a visitor is 2 places away.
- [ ] **WhatsApp Business API**: Push turn alerts and digital ticket passes directly to WhatsApp.
- [ ] **Multi-Language Support**: i18n support for Spanish, French, Arabic, and Hindi.

---

## Phase 2: Hardware & In-Store Kiosks
- [ ] **Waiting Hall TV Mode**: Fullscreen, high-contrast dashboard displaying currently called tokens with voice announcements.
- [ ] **Physical Ticket Printer Integration**: ESC/POS thermal printer support for issuing numbered paper tickets with QR codes at reception desks.
- [ ] **Self-Service Kiosk App**: Simplified tablet interface for walk-in visitor check-in.

---

## Phase 3: Advanced Analytics & AI Forecasting
- [ ] **Predictive Staffing Suggestions**: Recommendations for optimal counter openings based on historical hourly arrival surges.
- [ ] **Anomaly & No-Show Clustering**: Machine learning models identifying patrons likely to abandon long queues.
- [ ] **Enterprise Multi-Location SSO**: SAML 2.0 / Okta integration for hospital network staff onboarding.
